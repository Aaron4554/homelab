# ADR-0001: Purchase R720

- **Status:** Purchased
- **Date:** 16-Nov-2024

## Context
Friend was offering a good deal on his R720. I had some interest in homelabbing.

## Decision
Buy R720

## Consequences
+ Get experince running a small network.
+ A dedicated device to hold VMs and containers.

## Alternatives considered
- Buy more RaspberryPis: smaller, faster ram, but the storage, CPU cores, ram per $ doesn't come close.
